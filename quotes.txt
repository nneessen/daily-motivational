# The quotes the morning texts are built on. One per line: the quote, then " | ", then who said it.
#
# Every quote here was checked against a real source: Wikiquote's sourced quotes, Quote
# Investigator (quoteinvestigator.com), or the person's own book, speech or interview. A lot of
# famous quotes are pinned on the wrong person (most "Einstein", "Twain", "Churchill" and
# "Gandhi" lines are), so check a new one at quoteinvestigator.com or en.wikiquote.org first.
# Don't trust BrainyQuote, Goodreads or Pinterest for who said it.
#
# The program prints each quote exactly as written here. Claude only writes the line under it.
# Use plain " and ' quote marks and a plain hyphen, no curly quotes or long dashes.
# Each weekday takes the next four, so a quote doesn't come back until the whole list has been used.
